# SS rotary gear pump, L foot-mount version: 3D model spec

Built from the product photos only, not CAD. Each line says which photo confirms it. Sizes are read off photo 15 (near side-on) with the body taken as ⌀102 mm, about 4.3 px/mm, so expect ±5%. Where something can't be seen in any photo, it is marked **unconfirmed**.

Photos: 11, 12-1, 15, 16 (`public/images/*.webp`), plus `ss-rotary-gear-pump` and `ss-rotary-gear-pump-600`, which show the same pump as 16.

## Shaft (11, 12-1, 15)
- Black-oxide steel, ⌀16 mm, sticks out about 38 mm from the housing. (15)
- Chamfered end with a small centre-drill hole in the end face. (11)
- Keyway on top with a bright steel key with rounded ends, about 29 mm long, on the outer half. (11, 15)
- Enters the housing through a black oil seal sunk flush into the end face. (11)

## Bearing housing (11, 12-1, 15)
- Plain turned stainless cylinder, ⌀67 mm, about 37 mm long behind the plate. (15)
- Flat machined end face with a chamfered edge. (11, 12-1)
- ~~Two empty tapped holes~~ → **three** tapped holes at 120°, no screws (see corrections, item 3). (11)

## Bracket, cast iron painted black (11, 12-1, 15, 16)
- Upright plate about 20 mm thick, rough cast texture, satin black paint. (11, 16)
- **A-shaped outline:** the arched top follows the pump flange, about 20 mm above the body's top (radius ≈ 71); straight flanks splay outward down to the base. (16, 11)
- One hex bolt with a thick washer on the back of the plate, at the side of the housing. Others hidden, so the count is **unconfirmed**. (11)
- Base foot: a rectangle running along the pump from the plate to past the cover end, chamfered corners, **4 round holes** (2 per side, at two positions along it). (15, 12-1, 16)
- One central triangular rib under the pump, from the plate down to near the base end. (11, 12-1)

## Pump body, stainless (15, 16, 12-1, 11)
- **Front flange next to the plate:** turned, **larger than the body** (r ≈ 54.5 mm), about 20 mm long. (15)
- **Body:** r 51 mm, about 38 mm long.
  - Round on top: RZ mark, "Rotozest" reading across the body, and a double-outlined oblong mark with an arrow. (15, 12-1)
  - Flat machined pad around each port. Delivery port on the near side with "DELIVERY" in an oval above it; suction port on the far side with "SUCTION". (15, 16, 12-1)
  - Ports: ¾" BSP female, bright machined internal thread, chamfered entry. (16, 11, 12-1)
  - A small threaded hole beside each port. (16, 12-1)
- **Gear ring:** turned, shiny, about 15 mm long, one plain hole near the bottom on the nameplate side. (15, 16)
- Thin dark parting lines between flange, body and gear ring. (15)

## Cover and fasteners (16, 12-1, 15)
- Rear block in a **matte cast finish** (duller than the turned rings), rounded-rectangle, about 65 mm tall and 23 mm deep. (16, 15)
- **4 acorn nuts with hex bases and washers** on corner shoulders, pointing back along the shaft axis. (16, 12-1, 15)
- **Nameplate:** black, rounded corners, bonded (**no rivets**), on the block's end face. Text: RZ logo, "Rotozest", "ROTARY GEAR PUMP", then a two-column table (MODEL RZSS-075 · SIZE ¾"X¾"BSP · FLOW 33.33 LPM · PRESS 10 kg/cm² · SPEED 1440 RPM · SR.NO.), then "MFG. BY : ROTOZEST INDUSTRIES INDIA". (16)

## Not visible in any photo
